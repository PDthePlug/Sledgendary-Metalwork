Sledgendary Website Terms

Using this website or submitting an enquiry does not create a fabrication contract or payment obligation. Project-specific scope, pricing, validity, payment terms and other conditions are defined in the applicable quotation or project agreement.

Quotations

A quotation applies to the scope and validity period shown on that document. Changes to dimensions, materials, finish, design or site conditions may require a revised quotation.

Online acceptance

Where a quotation offers online acceptance, the recorded response applies to that specific quotation. Accepted commercial terms are locked in the Sledgendary system; later changes should be documented separately.

Structural work

Where professional engineering design, certification or approved structural specifications are required, fabrication and installation must follow the applicable professionally approved drawings or specifications.

Project-specific quotation and contract terms take precedence where they address the same subject more specifically.
