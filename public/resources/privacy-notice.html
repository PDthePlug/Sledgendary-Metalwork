Sledgendary Privacy Notice

Sledgendary processes personal information submitted through its website and business operations in order to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, manage projects, communicate with customers, invoice, record payments and maintain appropriate business records.

Information

This may include names, contact details, project locations, project briefs, correspondence, quotations, invoices and payment records.

Use and sharing

Sledgendary does not sell customer information. Service providers may process information where needed to host or operate the systems used by the business.

Your requests

Customers may request access or correction of personal information, or deletion where retention is no longer reasonably required for the project, legal obligations or legitimate business records. Use the contact details published on the Sledgendary website.

This notice should be reviewed as the company’s formal registration, information-officer details and service-provider arrangements are finalised.
