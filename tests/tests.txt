## Test 1 - Home creates a durable project enquiry [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: Home conversion, POST /api/enquiries, centralized business contact target, durable SLD reference
Description: Verifies the primary public customer journey still persists a real enquiry after the final integration sprint.

Steps:
1. Click "Start with an idea"
2. Choose "Metal Art", enter "Pride" as the name, "0712345678" as contact, "Stellenbosch" as location, and "A geometric protea wall sculpture for a wine estate entrance" as the idea
3. Click "Send project enquiry"

Expected: "PROJECT ENQUIRY RECEIVED" appears with a reference beginning "SLD-".

Description: Verifies the complete operating system, including Facebook/Instagram publishing controls and provider metadata, remains inaccessible to anonymous visitors.
